The Postgraduate Student Association of Universitas Gadjah Mada (HMP UGM) distributed 50 tanker loads of clean water, totaling about 250,000 liters, to drought-affected communities in Terbah Village, Gunungkidul Regency, Special Region of Yogyakarta, on Sunday (October 11). Norol Latifah, Chair of HMP UGM, expressed concern about the conditions Gunungkidul residents face during the dry season.
