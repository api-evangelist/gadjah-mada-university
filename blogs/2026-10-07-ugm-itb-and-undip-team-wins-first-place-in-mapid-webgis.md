Students from Universitas Gadjah Mada (UGM), Institut Teknologi Bandung (ITB), and Universitas Diponegoro (Undip), members of Team Cinajawabatak, won first place in the MAPID WebGIS Competition organized by MAPID with support from PT Kereta Api Indonesia (Persero). The competition was held at the Garuda Spark Innovation Hub, FX Sudirman, Jakarta, on Thursday (Sept. 24).
