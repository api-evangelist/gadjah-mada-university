The Faculty of Philosophy at Universitas Gadjah Mada (Philosophy UGM) hosted the Karawitan Festival and Nusantara Bazaar on its campus on Saturday (July 18) as part of its 59th anniversary celebration. The event featured performances by 52 gamelan ensembles and showcased 30 micro, small, and medium enterprises (MSMEs) selling food, accessories, and ornamental plants.
