Packaging does more than protect products from damage and contamination; it also shapes consumers’ first impressions. Therefore, packaging should be tailored to the product type, shelf life, and target market. Product labels must contain information that complies with applicable regulations, while branding establishes a product’s identity and distinguishes it from competitors.
