Indonesia has begun rolling out a policy restricting internet access for children under 16. Similar measures have already been introduced or proposed in several countries. Australia and Brazil have adopted restrictions on children’s access to social media, while similar policies are being discussed in parts of Europe and Asia, including Indonesia.
