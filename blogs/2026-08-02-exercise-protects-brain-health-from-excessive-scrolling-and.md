The growing popularity of exercise among young people may help counteract the sedentary habits associated with hours of scrolling. However, according to a Universitas Gadjah Mada (UGM) expert, protecting brain function depends not only on how intensely people exercise but also on how well the body maintains blood flow and oxygen supply to the brain.
