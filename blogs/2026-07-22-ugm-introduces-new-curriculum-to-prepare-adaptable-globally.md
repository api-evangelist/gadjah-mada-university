Universitas Gadjah Mada (UGM) will introduce a new university-wide curriculum in the first semester of the 2026/2027 academic year, as mandated by Rector Regulation No. 19 of 2025 on Education. Developed through extensive consultations with alumni, industry partners, and other stakeholders, the curriculum aims to align with workforce demands and global developments.
